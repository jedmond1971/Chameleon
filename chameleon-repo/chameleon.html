Your photos never leave this device. Everything happens right here in your browser — nothing is uploaded, stored, or shared with anyone. Ever.

We check what kind of file you actually have by looking inside it, not just by trusting its name — so even a mislabeled file (like a photo saved with the wrong extension) still converts correctly.

One more note: iPhone photos saved as HEIC are supported too. The first one you convert will take a few extra seconds while a small helper library downloads — that's just code, never your photo — and it's fast after that. You can also turn a GIF into another format, but not the other way around yet.
